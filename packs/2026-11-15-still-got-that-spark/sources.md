# Sources

These notes were built from one sermon: Pastor Sam, IFGF Bandung, first Sunday service, 15 November 2026, a message in the November series "Dreams and Visions" (YouTube livestream https://www.youtube.com/live/Y7FkceQFgZo, sermon at about 0:51:28 to 1:34:24). The pack has no practice material: no quiz, flashcards or mock exam, by the user's request. The preacher's three take-home questions appear in the synthesis chapter as reflection prompts, without answers.

The chain of work:

1. **Audio to text.** The service audio was transcribed with Whisper (large-v3-turbo). The unedited transcript of the sermon portion is `transcript.md`. Times in the notes are livestream times taken from it.
2. **Sermon map (`sermon-map.md`).** A first research pass read the whole transcript and wrote the outline with timestamps, the exact English taglines with the preacher's Indonesian beside them, the argument as a chain of reasons, his distinctions, transcription uncertainties and points to check against Scripture.
3. **Scripture check (`scripture-check.md`).** A second research pass identified every passage he read, retold or alluded to, fetched the exact text (ESV, NIV, NKJV and MSG from Bible Gateway; TB from alkitab.mobi), noted TB versification, and wrote a fit check for each use.
4. **Writing.** `walkthrough.html` was written from those two files, with wording checked against `transcript.md`. Every verse block in the walkthrough was compared by script against the text in `scripture-check.md` (ignoring only straight versus curly quotation marks) and all match.

Translations quoted: ESV (Crossway), TB Terjemahan Baru (Lembaga Alkitab Indonesia), NIV (Biblica), The Message (NavPress), NKJV (Thomas Nelson, used for comparison only).

## Conflicts / Points needing care

Where the sermon and the wording of Scripture differ, the walkthrough says what the preacher said, quotes the text, and judges whether his point still stands, in short "Reading it carefully" boxes. Each comes from the fit checks in scripture-check.md.

- **Grape cluster** (back-story): "several people" carried it; Numbers 13:23 says two. Point unaffected.
- **"Wherever you set your foot"** (back-story; again in the prayer, synthesis): not in Numbers 13 to 14; the general promise is Deuteronomy 11:24 and Joshua 1:3, Caleb's is Joshua 14:9. The land becomes Israel's or Caleb's, not "God's inheritance".
- **Reward "because you brought good news"** (caleb-request): Joshua 14:9 grounds the oath in wholehearted following.
- **"It's not talking about physical"** (caleb-request): Joshua 14:11 is about physical strength for war; he first said "not physical only", which the notes follow.
- **The giants promise** (caleb-request): he hedges ("maybe it is not written"); recorded promises concern the land; Joshua 15:14 records the outcome.
- **Gilgal** (caleb-request): "on the mountains of Jericho"; Joshua 4:19 puts it on the east border of Jericho, in the Jordan valley.
- **"Eyes are the window of the heart"** (spark): a saying, not a verse (Matthew 6:22 differs).
- **Joseph as "a small child"** (spark): Genesis 37:2 gives his age as 17.
- **Deuteronomy 34:7 in The Message** (excitement): "spring in his step" is the paraphrase; eyesight read spiritually; Deuteronomy 31:2 noted for balance.
- **Psalm 37:23** (excitement): his wording is closest to NKJV; "steps" means the course of life.
- **Elijah, 1 Kings 19** (excitement): order compressed; the question is asked at Horeb (19:9, 13), after the angel feeds him (19:5-7) and the 40-day journey (19:8). "Offended at God" is interpretation; the text says he was afraid (19:3).
- **Joseph's "I have joy"** (excitement): the preacher's dramatisation.
- **David and Goliath as "a small child"** (excitement): the text calls him a youth. Not boxed; mentioned only by quoting "young David".
- **2 Samuel 11:1** (excitement): Uriah is not named in 11:1; boredom is the preacher's reading.
- **Kenite versus Kenizzite** (expectation): Caleb is a Kenizzite (Kenas); Moses' father-in-law's clan were Kenites (Keni); "same tribe as Moses' father-in-law" is not supported; "adopted" is not the text's word. The TB calls Hobab "ipar Musa" in Judges 1:16 and 4:11 where ESV has father-in-law; this does not affect the point and is not discussed in the book.
- **"45 years in the wilderness"** (expectation): about 38 in the wilderness, the rest in the conquest.
- **Moses "did not see"** (expectation): he corrects himself at once to "saw from afar". The reason Moses did not enter (Numbers 20:12) is not discussed in the sermon and is left out of the book.
- **"Going out and coming in"** (experience): the idiom means active leadership and battle; the tabernacle sense is his application.
- **Mount Hermon** (experience): not in Joshua 14; his illustration.
- **"Caleb was Moses' disciple"** (synthesis): Scripture names Joshua as Moses' assistant.
- **Deuteronomy 28:13 in the prayer** (synthesis): the verse is conditional on obedience.
- **"Faith without works is useless"** (synthesis): closest to James 2:20 (ESV "useless"; TB "kosong"); 2:26 has "dead".
- **Nehemiah 8:10** (synthesis): TB numbers it 8:11 and has "perlindunganmu" (your refuge) for "your strength".
